A little about me

I believe that great design is invisible. It's the seamless flow that empowers people to achieve their goals without thinking about the tool.

With over a decade of experience, I've partnered with visionary founders and global enterprises to translate complex problems into intuitive digital ecosystems. My approach is rooted in deep user research, iterative prototyping, and a relentless focus on outcomes over output.

Outside of work, I'm usually rebuilding old furniture, running slow, or reading about systems that have nothing to do with software.
