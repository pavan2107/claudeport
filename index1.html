(ABOUT ME)

My Journey & Craft

I'm a product designer who likes turning messy, ambiguous problems into interfaces people actually enjoy using. Over the last 7+ years, I've worked across fintech, healthtech, and consumer apps — moving from hands-on UI work to leading design across full product lifecycles.

My process leans heavily on research and iteration: understanding the real behavior behind a request, prototyping fast, and refining with data rather than opinions. I care as much about the system behind a product as I do about the pixel — design tokens, component libraries, and handoff quality all matter to me.

Outside of client and product work, I mentor early-career designers and occasionally speak on design systems and product thinking. (Placeholder bio — replace with your real story.)
